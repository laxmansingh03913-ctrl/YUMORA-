Feed Bhilwara ke baare me

Feed Bhilwara Bhilwara shahar, Rajasthan ka ek seva-bhavi sangathan hai. Humara ek hi mission hai — koi bhookha na soye.

Hamari team roz zarooratmand bachchon aur parivaron tak garam khana pahunchati hai, blood donation camp lagati hai, aur mushkil me fase logon ka saath deti hai.

⭐ Google pe 4.6 rating — Bhilwara ke logon ka pyaar aur bharosa hamari sabse badi kamai hai.

🤝 Aap bhi jud sakte hain — donate karke, volunteer bankar, ya ann-daan me sahyog dekar.
