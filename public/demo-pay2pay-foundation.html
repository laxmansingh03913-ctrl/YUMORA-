Foundation ke baare me

Pay2pay Social Foundation Jaipur (Mansarovar) ki ek women-led samaj-sevi sanstha hai. Humara mukhya uddeshya hai — Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao.

Anathalaya sahayata yojana, kapda recycling, aur shiksha-jagrukta ke madhyam se hum samaj ke kamzor vargon tak madad pahunchate hain.
